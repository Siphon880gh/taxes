Any other deductions that are not tied to a section goes to a section Misc Deductions. Credits go to their own sections as well unless not tied to a section, then it's named "Misc Credits". The skill should also add other summary sections that are needed. Sections that dont apply, you can add them with a "N/A" underneath.
