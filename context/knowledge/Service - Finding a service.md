
**Mode**

You can work with a tax preparer in person or remote. H&R offers both.

You can use your own tax software that generates the form

---

**Pricing and Involvement**

Some companies charge by the form.

H&R block has Form 1040 free only with simple tax situations. Other forms cost. You work with in-person or remote tax preparer.

Turbotax has Form 1040 free. Other forms cost. By default it's self-service, unless you pay for a remote tax expert.

FreeTaxUSA has all forms free for federal. State costs $14.99. By default it's self-service and no amends. Deluxe for $14.99 for unlimited amends and live chat. Pro adds phone support and live screen sharing (so it doesn't seem to be seamless where the tax expert can view your files on their side). Audit defense for $19.99. They also offer various forms of hard-copies.
