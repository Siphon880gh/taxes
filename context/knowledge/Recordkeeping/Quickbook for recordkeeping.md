## Quickbook Recordkeeping Features

You can sync your bank to Quickbook. It can automatically record your income and expenses by setting up rules to automatically categorize transactions from your bank statements into your income statements. For electronic receipts, you can easily upload them directly into Quickbooks or email them from your email address. For paper receipts, you can use the Quickbook app to take a picture and it'll pick up the numbers. Quick book can track mileage

## Pricing

QuickBooks Solopreneur: Designed for sole proprietors and freelancers, this plan offers features like income and expense tracking, mileage tracking, and integration with TurboTax for tax preparation. After the 30-day free trial, it costs $20 per month. ​
https://quickbooks.intuit.com/solopreneur/

QuickBooks Online Simple Start: Suitable for basic bookkeeping needs, this plan includes features like invoicing and receipt capture. It starts at $35 per month, with a 50% discount for the first three months if you skip the free trial. 
https://quickbooks.intuit.com/oa/get-quickbooks/
