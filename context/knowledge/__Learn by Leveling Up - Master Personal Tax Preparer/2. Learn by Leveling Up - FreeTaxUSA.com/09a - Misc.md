Of particular note is **Installation Form**. But there are other ways to apply for installation plans.

Another noteworthy item is **Underpayment Penalty (Form 2210)**
- Clicking Underpayment Penalty from the screen goes to this screen
- If you had paid quarterly fees as a non-W2 worker that didn't have income withhold (employer paying quarterly IRS taxes), then fill this in with a "YES" so you can defend your position to not be charged. But if you know you will be hit with the full underpayment penalty, because you didn't pay ahead of the $0 balance every quarter and you worked for income without being W2 withheld income, choose "No".
