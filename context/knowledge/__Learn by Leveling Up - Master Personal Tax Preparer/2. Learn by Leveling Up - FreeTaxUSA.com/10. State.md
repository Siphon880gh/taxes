^ **“Was the property you rented exempt from property tax in 2024?”**
They’re asking because some tax credits (like renter’s credit in California) are only available if the property you rented was _not_ exempt from property tax — meaning the landlord did pay property tax on it. In nearly all cases, you should answer “No” — meaning that the property was not exempted from property tax and that your landlord paid property tax which is the normal.

^ Why this matters:
- If your landlord is **a nonprofit, church, or government agency**, they **might** not pay property tax. In that case, **you wouldn’t qualify** for certain state credits like the **California Renter’s Credit**.
- But if you're renting from a **private landlord or property management company**, it’s safe to assume **they do pay property tax**, and your answer should be **"No, it was not exempt."**
