Yes, FreeTaxUSA generates the various forms like 1040, Schedule C, etc for you, and you can preview them before finishing filing. There's the final preview of the entire tax return before you file. And there are pitstop previews where there's a button to generate the Schedule C so far (the screen where you get to cherry pick what types of income to go into Schedule C, the preview button is at the bottom of all the possible cherry pick options)

Freetaxusa works mostly by asking straight questionnaire, sometimes asking you to enter exactly the inputs at line X or box Y on your tax statement (eg. W2 or 1995-NEC), and at worse it has a screen of subcategories for you to cherry pick (Like "Rental Income (Schedule E)")

So it helps to know the reasoning behind why those numbers are generated at the PDF previews and to ensure accuracy still.

That's why it's recommended you first master personal taxes first by completing the other Level Up series.

At worse, you could use another tax software that generates PDFs in tandem with FreeTaxUSA to ensure accuracy. But at best, you mastered Personal Taxes by following the Level Up Series, so you can tell if FreeTaxUSA is generating the PDFs correctly.
