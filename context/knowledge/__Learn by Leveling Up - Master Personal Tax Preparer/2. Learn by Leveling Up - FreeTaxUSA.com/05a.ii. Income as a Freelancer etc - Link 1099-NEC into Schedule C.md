In FreeTaxUSA, when you "link" your 1099-NEC into Schedule C, you're rolling in the 1099-NEC income you reported to FreeTaxUSA at an earlier page.

Requirement: You transferred information from Form 1099-NEC into freetaxusa.com already. You should have if you went down in order from top to bottom, but the Income categories screen lets people skip around:
(Required to have completed a 1099-NEC)
![[Pasted image 20250416232435.png]]

You should definitely link the 1099-NEC into Schedule C, so there's a breadcrumb where that income is from (especially if you mix incomes that you couldn't get 1099-NEC's for).

You have to include 1099-NEC into Schedule C because technically that’s you being a freelancer or contractor or business owner. You weren’t classified as an employee (one who has someone else control their manner of work, time, and place), yet you worked for an income. Schedule C is working for income and profits that is not the traditional employee (aka W2 worker)

First, make sure you're working on a Schedule C. You would have clicked this:
