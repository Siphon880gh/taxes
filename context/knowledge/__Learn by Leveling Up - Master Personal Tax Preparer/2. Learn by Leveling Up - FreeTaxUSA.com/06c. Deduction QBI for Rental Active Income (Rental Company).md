###  🛑 STOP

Note this does **NOT** apply to small landlords renting out to low volume of tenants (eg. one family) because it doesn't necessitate day-to-day operations of a rental business. This only applies to a big firm who does the day-to-day operations directly. Does not apply to rich investors who hire a management firm to carry the day-to-day operations because that becomes passive rental income too!
