## Your Owed Tax

IRS offers yearly a PDF that shows:
- the **federal income tax owed** based on **taxable income** and **filing status** for tax year 2024
- the **earned income credit** that you can gain when meeting criteria (criteria that you're working freelance or W2, you're living in the US, and that you're of working age)

Every year you have to find the newest PDF:
https://www.irs.gov/forms-instructions

![[Pasted image 20250412015829.png]]

Eg. This is 2024's:
https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i1040tt.pdf

---

First half of the PDF is to find your tax bracket's owe tax. 
![[Pasted image 20250412015505.png]]

**How to read it:**

- **Income Range Columns** ("At Least" and "But Less Than"):
    - This shows the taxable income range. For example, if your taxable income is between **$25,300 and $25,350**, you look at the row for that range.
        
- **Filing Status Columns**:
    - "Single", "Married filing jointly", "Married filing separately", and "Head of household" are different filing statuses. The number under each column is the **tax owed** for that income range and filing status.

**Example:**
- If your **taxable income** is **$25,320** and your **filing status** is **Married Filing Jointly**, your tax is **$2,575** (as circled in the table).
