Besides the incomes and costs you readily can file, you want to check if your employers, partners, etc have sent documents to IRS involving you. Instead of asking them, you can login to your IRS account to view tax files that have been filed involving you at the "Return Documents" page.

You must address all these Returned Documents in your tax filing. A mismatch here is an easy way for IRS to automatically flag you for auditing.

Refer to [[IRS Account and ID.me]] on how to sign up or login.

Returned Documents page is at:
- The url [https://sa.www4.irs.gov/ola/information_return](https://sa.www4.irs.gov/ola/information_return)

Here, I know I need to report Interest Income and W2 from Company LLC. 

For example, Company LLC had a massive laid off in the first month in 2024. They still reported I earned a few hundred dollars to the IRS despite laying off my entire department after several days into the year. 

But they never mailed or emailed me a W2. I didn't even remember I had some income from the first few days of the year 2024. If I hadn't checked Returned Documents, I wouldn't have file the income (especially since the Company LLC has shuttered and never mailed or emailed W2 to me). It would have risked my taxes being flagged to be audited.
