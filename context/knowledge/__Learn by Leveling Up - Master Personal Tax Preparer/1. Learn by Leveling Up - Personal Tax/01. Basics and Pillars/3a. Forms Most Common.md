## Basics

Most people need to fill form 1040, even if they're only contractor (no employee W2's)

Form 1040 are usually the first pages you attach on your file return. You may have additional Schedule 1-3 and/or Schedule A-..

Those Schedules are known to be extensions of Form 1040. So a Schedule A could be referred as Form 1040 Schedule A. This is nice to know when using the form search engine at IRS.gov if not using tax software / web app that automatically selects and fills in forms for you as you answer questions.

It's still good to know all the forms and schedules so that you can conceptualize how much you're filing and how complete you are at filing.

## Common Forms

W4 - Employer to be able to withhold income for you
W2 - Employees only. May have withheld income. This prevents underpayment penalty (having to pay IRS every quarter ahead of $0 balance at irs.gov)

1099-NEC 
1099-K generated from business accounts where you make electronic transactions, eg. Venmo, etc

1040 - Quick snapshot of income, profits, and their net tax liability from subtracting deductions and credits. Remember the goal of IRS is to make taxes fair between government and the taxpayer and to also prevent people from gaming the system. Form 1040 often have lines that refer to other schedules or forms.

Schedule 1 - More adjustments (subtracting from taxes etc)
Schedule 2 - More taxes (Self employment tax, etc)
Schedule 3 - More credits (Net premium tax credit)

Schedule A - Itemized deductions (if your itemized deductions do not exceed standard deductions). 
- Mnemonic: Item A, Item B, Item C.

Schedule B - Dividends and interests
- Mnemonic: Bonds

Schedule C - Profits and Loss of Business (Should include 1099-NEC)
- Mnemonic: Profits and **C**osts of Business
- Mnemonic: You can start your own business or sorta stand by your own business being a contractor or a freelancer (not as controlled as an employee) but there’s a **C**ost to business

Schedule D - Capital Gains and Losses
- Indication: Withdraw from stocks, crypto, etc into bank
- Mnemonic: Dow Jones, Defi crypto markets

Schedule E - Supplemental Income and Losses
- Indication: Rental income, Loyalties, partnerships / S corporations
- Mnemonic: **E**Z/easy passive income. These are mostly passive incomes where you're not that involved minute to minute like employee or freelance or contracter work.

8995 - Qualified Business Income
- Helps deduct 20% off your 1099-NEC income, and your other incomes/profits that you couldn't get a 1099-NEC from
	- **Don't get tripped up**: Schedule C includes 1099-NEC income, your other incomes. It could include rental income if you're a big firm (Renting to one family is not a big firm because it's not heavily day-to-day operations, and it's cheaper to have renting as passive income in only Schedule E)
- Mnemonic: Helps qualify 1099-NEC (99 is in the middle). As for the 8 and 5, just think of it as decreasing the total tax liability (8 to 5 is a decrease in number).
