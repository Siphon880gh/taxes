Do you buy your own health insurance? This is usually the case if you work solely as a freelancer / contractor / business owner instead of a W2 employee who has health insurance through their employer.

And did you use the state or federal assisted Health Insurance Marketplaces to buy health insurance cover? This is usually the case if health insurance is too expensive for you and you want a discounted rate. This is more the case for a freelancer or someone who's just starting their small business (Not so much the case for a successful business owner).

Living in California or most other blue states, you MUST pay healthcare monthly (else get penalized when filing the state tax return), which is expensive every month so you may apply through state or federally government assisted health insurance marketplace. 

For California, the online marketplace is CoveredCalifornia. 

All marketplaces do this:
	- They give you a rebate every month to cover the premium costs as long as you qualified based on your income level. 
	- At the end of the year, Covered California generated a tax statement 1095 A. 
	- If you were actually not given enough rebates and you foot the bill higher than usual during the year for health insurance, on Form 1040, you earned \*Premium Tax Credit
