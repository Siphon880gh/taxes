
#### ✅ Step 1: Gather Your W-2

Each employer sends you a **Form W-2**. It shows:
- Your total wages
- Taxes withheld (federal, Social Security, Medicare, and state)
- Employer info and your SSN
#### ✅ Step 2: Transfer W-2 Info to 1040

| **W-2 Box** | **Transfer To**                 | **Form**                                                               | **Line on 1040**            |
| ----------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Box 1       | Wages, tips, other comp         | **Form 1040**                                                          | **Line 1a**                 |
| Box 2       | Federal income tax withheld     | **Form 1040**                                                          | **Line 25a**                |
| Box 3 + 5   | Just reference amounts withheld | Not reported separately (used by IRS)                                  |                             |
| Box 4       | Social Security tax withheld    | For checking overpayment – not input manually unless too much withheld |                             |
| Box 6       | Medicare tax withheld           | Same as Box 4 – IRS already tracks this                                |                             |
| Box 16      | State wages                     | **State return**                                                       | Input on **state tax form** |
| Box 17      | State income tax withheld       | **State return**                                                       | Input on **state tax form** |

> 💡 You **do not** manually enter Social Security or Medicare tax withheld unless there was too much taken out — the IRS uses W-2s behind the scenes.

#### ✅ Step 3: Attach W-2 to Your Return

- If **e-filing**: Enter the W-2 information in your tax software. It will auto-fill Form 1040.
- If **mailing**: Attach **Copy B** of your W-2 to the front of your Form 1040.

---

### 📎 Do You Need Any Other Forms?

- **Schedule 1**: If you have adjustments to income (e.g. student loan interest, HSA contributions).
- **Schedule 3**: If you're claiming credits (e.g. education, dependent care).
- **State Return**: You'll need to report **Box 16–17** info here.
