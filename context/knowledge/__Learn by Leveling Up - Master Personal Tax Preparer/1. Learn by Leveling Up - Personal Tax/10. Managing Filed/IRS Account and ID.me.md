## Do I need the account?

You don't need an account to:
- [make a payment](https://www.irs.gov/payments "Payments")
- [check your refund](https://www.irs.gov/wheres-my-refund "Where's my refund?")
- [check your amended return](https://www.irs.gov/filing/wheres-my-amended-return "Where’s My Amended Return?")

But it certainly makes it easier to do the tasks above to have an online IRS account
