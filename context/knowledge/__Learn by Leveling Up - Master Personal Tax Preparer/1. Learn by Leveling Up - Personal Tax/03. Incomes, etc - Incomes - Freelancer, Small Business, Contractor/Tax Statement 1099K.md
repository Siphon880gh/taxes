You should already know that tax statements are generated from your partners or companies when doing business with you as you the freelancer / contractor. You learned this in an earlier level tutorial [[4a. Tax Statements and Returned Documents]].

_Form 1099-K_ is a form that reports credit and debit card sales revenue for your business. _Form 1099-K_ can be sent to anyone who uses payment apps or online marketplaces to accept payments for selling goods or providing services. This includes people with side hustles, small businesses, crafters and other sole proprietors.

Could be to Business Income (Schedule C) in most cases. However, it depends on the nature of the transactions reported on the 1099-K.
