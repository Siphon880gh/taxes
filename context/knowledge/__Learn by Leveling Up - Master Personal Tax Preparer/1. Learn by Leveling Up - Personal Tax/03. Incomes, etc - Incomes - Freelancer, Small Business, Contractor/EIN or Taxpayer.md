On Schedule C, you see on the right the SSN and the EIN.
![[Pasted image 20250414234939.png]]

On **Schedule C**, you'll see spaces for both your **Social Security Number (SSN)** and **Employer Identification Number (EIN)**.
- If your business **does not have an EIN**, simply leave that field **blank**.
- The IRS will use your **SSN** as the taxpayer ID for the business.

An **EIN** is used to identify a business as a separate tax entity. While it's not required for all sole proprietors, getting one can help **separate your business and personal finances**, and it's **necessary if you have employees or cofounders**.
