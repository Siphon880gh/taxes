At various forms including Form 8995 for QBI deduction, you may be asked to fill the taxpayer identification number for the associated business.
#### ✅ **If you're a sole proprietor or own rental property in your own name**:
- Use **your Social Security Number (SSN)** as the **Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)**.
- This applies to most **individual landlords** and freelancers.
- Even if you registered a "doing business as" (DBA) name, you still use your **SSN** unless you’ve obtained an EIN and file taxes under it.

#### ✅ **If the business is an LLC or partnership with its own EIN**:
- Use the **Employer Identification Number (EIN)** — but **only if** that business files its own return (like a partnership filing Form 1065), and **you’re reporting QBI from that pass-through entity** via a **K-1**.
