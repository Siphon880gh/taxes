
If you worked from home, you can deduct utilities including water, electricity, cellphone, internet, etc but only that portion for which you worked instead of daily living activities. So you deduct proportionally.

^ It gets complicated if you don't work consistently. Easier if you work whole months in the year. Harder if you work a few days out of a month and not on all months. See bottom for example of the harder case.

If you consider a part of your home as your personal office, you can deduct proportionally with the home insurance, taxes,  mortgage, etc. Ask: What percentage of your home out of your total property is used for office work.
