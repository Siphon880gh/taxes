You are asking here:
Can my w2 income, 1099 nec income, and/or Schedule C self owned business incomes, and/or rent income have qualified business income deductions? That's usually 20% deduction to each income that qualifies for QBI deduction

Rent income is tricky. It's active rental income if it's a day-to-day operation (you aren't a rich investor that paid management firm, and you aren't a small landlord that rented out to a small volume of tenants like one family which means only occasionally you have to deal with renting operations). Otherwise it's passive rental income and you can't get deductions for it. You wouldn't list it as an income in Schedule C and instead you list it in Schedule E. Passive incomes in Schedule E cannot be deducted with QBI.
