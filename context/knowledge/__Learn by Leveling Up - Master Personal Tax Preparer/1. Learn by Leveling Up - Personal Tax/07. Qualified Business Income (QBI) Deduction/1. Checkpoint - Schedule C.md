
### **So to answer your question:**
- **1099-NEC** income goes on **Schedule C** (because it’s considered **self-employed** income).
- Work you've done that got paid for but couldn't get a 1099-NEC from the client - that's also rolled into Schedule C.
- **Rental income** goes on **Schedule E**, **not** on Schedule C.

> You do **not** mix rental income with your freelance/business income, unless you’re running a **hotel or B&B** (in which case the IRS may treat it more like a business).
