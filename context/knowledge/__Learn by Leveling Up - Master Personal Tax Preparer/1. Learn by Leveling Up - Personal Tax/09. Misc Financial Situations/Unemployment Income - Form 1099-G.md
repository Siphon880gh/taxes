Form 1099-G is a tax document issued by government agencies like the **Employment Development Department (EDD)** to report **taxable income** paid to you during the calendar year. This includes **unemployment benefits**, **Paid Family Leave**, **Disability Insurance (when paid instead of unemployment)**, and other government payments.

The **total amount you received** is reported to the **IRS**, and **you must include it when filing your taxes**. Even if you were paid for only a short period (infrequent or irregular unemployment), it's still reportable income.

- Box 1 of the form shows **unemployment compensation**.
- Box 10a shows the **state** that paid the benefits.
- If federal income tax was withheld, it appears in **Box 4**.
