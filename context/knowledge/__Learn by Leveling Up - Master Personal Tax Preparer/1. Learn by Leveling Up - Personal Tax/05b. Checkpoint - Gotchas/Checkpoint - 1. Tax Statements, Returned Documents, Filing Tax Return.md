
**Tax Statements** are the W2, 1099-NECs, 1095-A, etc you get from your employer, company you work with, company your a customer with. You use these statements to file your tax return

**Returned Documents** are forms that your employer or company you're working with electronically submit to IRS, hence you need to be aware of them, because you certainly need to file your tax return about them, so you don't get flagged for auditing:
- View returned documents under your IRS account [https://sa.www4.irs.gov/ola/information_return](https://sa.www4.irs.gov/ola/information_return)

Filing your **Tax Return**

It's called a tax return because technically you've been paying the IRS every quarter. As a W2 employee, that withold income is paid to the IRS every quarter. If you're a freelancer/contractor/business, you should've been paying quarterly ahead of the $0 balance. Then at the end of the Tax Year and several months later, deadline April 15th, you perform a final filing of your income, deductions, and credits, to see if you overpaid or underpaid. Then either you pay or you get refunded. At a rare chance, neither, if it all balanced out.

Mnemonic: At end of the year you file information that could return to you a refund or ask you more (taxes due). It’s a **returnable** document at end of tax year.
