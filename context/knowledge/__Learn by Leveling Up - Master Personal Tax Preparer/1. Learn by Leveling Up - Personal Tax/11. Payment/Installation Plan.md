
You may qualify to apply online if:

- Long-term payment plan (installment agreement): You owe $50,000 or less in combined tax, penalties and interest. You have filed all required returns.
- Short-term payment plan: You owe less than $100,000 in combined tax, penalties and interest.

Note if you are a **sole proprietor** or **independent contractor,** apply for a payment plan as an individual.

More details: https://www.irs.gov/payments/online-payment-agreement-application

You may also apply through a form:
- Form 9465 - Installment Agreement Request  
- Use this form to request a monthly installment plan if you cannot pay the full amount you owe shown on your tax return (or on a notice we sent you).

## Credit Score?

The IRS does not check your credit when you apply for an installment agreement to pay your tax debt. You just have to qualify (the criteria above).

IRS does not report the installment agreement to credit bureaus. Therefore, entering into an IRS payment plan and being consistent on payment does not help your credit score.

However, if you default on the agreement—such as by missing payments—the IRS may take collection actions, including filing a Notice of Federal Tax Lien. While the IRS does not report to credit bureaus, a federal tax lien becomes public record and can be discovered by lenders, potentially impacting your ability to obtain credit.

## Example

If you owe **$1,600** to the IRS and want to set up an **installment plan** (payment plan), here's what it typically looks like:

---

### ✅ **Eligibility**

For $1,600 in tax debt:
- You're eligible for a **short-term** or **long-term** payment plan.
- No need to submit a financial statement if under $50,000 total owed (tax + penalties + interest).
    

---

### 🔹 **Two Main Options**

#### 1. **Short-Term Payment Plan (up to 180 days)**

- No setup fee.
- Pay the full $1,600 within 180 days.
- Penalties and interest still accrue until paid off.
- Can pay through IRS Direct Pay, debit/credit card, check, or money order.
    

**Example:**  
Paying $400/month for 4 months = $1,600  
(plus a small amount of interest/penalty)

---

#### 2. **Long-Term Payment Plan (monthly installment)**

- Setup fee:
	- **$31** if you apply online and use **Direct Debit** from bank.
	- **$130** if you apply by phone/mail/in person and pay another way.
- Penalties & interest continue to accrue (~0.5% penalty + interest monthly).
- Spreads payments over up to 72 months (but for $1,600, you’d probably choose 6–12 months).
    

**Example:**  
If you choose to pay over **12 months**:
- $1,600 ÷ 12 = **~$134/month**
- Add ~$3–5/month interest and penalty.
- Total cost over the year could be around **$1,650–$1,700**.

---

### ⚠️ **Penalty & Interest Breakdown**

Here’s how the additional costs accumulate while on a payment plan:

|**Type**|**Rate**|**Notes**|
|---|---|---|
|**Failure-to-pay penalty**|~0.5% of the unpaid tax per month (max 25%)|Reduced to 0.25% per month _if_ on an active installment plan|
|**Interest**|Federal short-term rate + 3%|Compounded daily. Approx ~8% annually in recent years|

**On $1,600**, expect:

- ~**$4–$7/month** combined penalty + interest
    
- If paid off in 6–12 months, total extra cost is **~$30–$100**

---

### 🔍 **How to Apply**

- Online: [IRS Payment Plan Application](https://www.irs.gov/payments/online-payment-agreement-application)
- By phone: Call 800-829-1040
- Or submit **Form 9465** (Installment Agreement Request)
