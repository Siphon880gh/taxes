### 👉 Filing an Extension = More Time to File, **Not** More Time to Pay

When you file a **tax extension** (Form 4868), it gives you **more time to submit your tax return** (typically until October 15).  But it **does not extend your deadline to pay** any taxes you owe.


### 🛑 If You Owe Taxes and Don't File a Tax Return:
- You are **still required to pay** if no estimate is made by you filing a tax return
- If you **don’t pay** by then:
    - You may face **interest** on the unpaid amount.
    - You may also face a **failure-to-pay penalty**.
    - You may also face a **failure-to-file penalty**.

### 🛑 If You Owe Taxes and File an Extension:
- You are **still required to pay** what you estimate you owe **by the original due date** (usually **April 15**).
- If you **don’t pay** by then:
    - You may face **interest** on the unpaid amount.
    - You may also face a **failure-to-pay penalty**.

### Two Main Charges If You Don’t Pay On Time:
#### 1. **Failure-to-Pay Penalty**
- **0.5% per month** of the unpaid tax
- Capped at **25%** of what you owe
- This is charged **every month** (or part of a month) your payment is late
#### 2. **Interest on Unpaid Taxes**
- Charged **on top of the penalty**
- Rate is variable: it changes quarterly and is currently around **8% annually (or ~0.66% per month)** as of early 2025
- Interest starts **the day after the due date** (e.g., April 16)

### 🔍 Example: You Owe $2,500 but Don't Pay by April 15

Let’s say you don’t pay and file in **October** — 6 months late:

|Type|Calculation|Amount|
|---|---|---|
|Failure-to-Pay|0.5% × $2,500 × 6 months|$75|
|Interest (~8% APR)|Approx. $2,500 × 0.66% × 6 months|~$99|
|**Total Extra Owed**|Penalty + Interest|**$174**|

So you’d owe **$2,500 + $174 = $2,674** after 6 months of delay.

### ⚠️ And if you also didn’t file on time (without an extension)?

You’d get hit with the **Failure-to-File penalty**, which is way worse:
- **5% per month** (instead of 0.5%) for the first 5 months
- Can also stack with the failure-to-pay penalty

💣 So: **file on time** or file an **extension** — but **always try to pay something** by April 15 if you might owe.
