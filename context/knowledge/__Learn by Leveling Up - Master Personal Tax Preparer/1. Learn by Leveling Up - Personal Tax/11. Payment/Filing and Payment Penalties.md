
### 🚨 1. **Failure-to-File Penalty**  
If you **don’t file** your return on time:

- **5% of the unpaid taxes per month** (or part of a month)
- **Capped at 25% total**
- If your return is **over 60 days late**, the **minimum penalty** is the lesser of:
  - $485 (for returns due in 2024), or  
  - 100% of the tax due

> 💡 Example: Owe $2,000 and file 3 months late?  
> Penalty = $2,000 × 5% × 3 months = **$300**

---

### 🕒 2. **Failure-to-Pay Penalty**  
If you **filed on time** but didn’t pay:

- **0.5% per month** of the unpaid tax
- Also capped at **25%**
- This penalty **continues even if you’re on a payment plan**, though it drops to **0.25% per month** if you are

> 💡 Owe $2,000 and paid 6 months late?  
> Penalty = $2,000 × 0.5% × 6 = **$60**

---

### ⚖️ 3. **Underpayment Penalty (Estimated Taxes)**  
This is where your **daily 7% interest** comes in — no flat fee here, just **interest accrued daily** if:

- You didn’t pay enough during the year through withholding or estimated tax payments

> This one’s calculated with daily compounding — no 5%/0.5% type monthly penalties.

---

### 🧠 Summary

| Type of Issue                 | Penalty                                 |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Filed late                   | 5%/month, capped at 25%                  |
| Paid late                    | 0.5%/month, capped at 25%                |
| Underpaid during the year    | ~7%/year interest, compounded daily      |
