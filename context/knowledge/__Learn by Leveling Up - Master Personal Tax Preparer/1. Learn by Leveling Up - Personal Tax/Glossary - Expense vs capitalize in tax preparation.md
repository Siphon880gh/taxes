Wheen you **capitalize** a purchase in accounting or taxes, you're saying: "This thing is valuable and will last a while—so I won’t deduct it all now. I’ll spread the cost out over several years."

But if you **expense** the purchase instead, that's all handled at that one tax form instead of spreading across multiple tax years. You are taking the full deduction now.
- It's usually because of a Safe Harbor rule (specifically [[De Minimis Safe Harbor]]) that says if the purchase is small enough in price, why don't we take care of it all at the tax year it was purchased.

Similar to capitalizing over tax years, depreciation which often is a deduction, is also over tax years.
