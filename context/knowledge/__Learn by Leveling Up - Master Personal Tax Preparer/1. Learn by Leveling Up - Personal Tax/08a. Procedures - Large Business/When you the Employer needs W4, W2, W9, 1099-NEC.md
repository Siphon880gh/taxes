Generally when you have to pay your worker(s) >=$600, you have to start issuing W2 or 1099-NEC

---

Note W4 certificate is required to perform withholding of incomes for employees (legal definition of employee is you control the manner they work, where, and the time they work) which is reported on the tax document W2. Have employee sign the certificate which includes how much is withheld. Usually done before or at the time of hire.

Then by a deadline but within a timeframe, you issue the employee a W2 tax statement that they can use to help file taxes.

---

For independent contractors working with you, give them a Form W-9 to sign and to hand to you, so you know who you're paying and their TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number). Aided by this information, later you can issue them a 1099-NEC (Non employee compensation) tax statement, by a deadline but within a timeframe, to help them file taxes. You do not withhold income for them.

---

Mnemonics:
- W2, W4. They're both even numbered. For employer-employee.
- W9, 1099-NEC (Non employee compensation). They have the 9 as the final digit.
