Schedule E (Rental Income) - Line 17 (Utilities)
![[Pasted image 20250414184520.png]]

Are you paying water on shared water meter? Meaning the tenants don't have their own water bill? Then you can take the portion of the water bill that's equivalent to the number of tenants to total residents on the shared water bill.

For example, Property 1 and Property 2 share the same water bill.
Property 1 is where the landlord is living in. There are only two residents here.
Property 2 is where the tenants live and there are 4.

Therefore 4/6 is 0.67 and that's how much the water bills you should deduct on Schedule E (Rental Income). Let's say for the tax year, the water bill sums to $1656.83, then proportionalized at 27%, it's $447.34. You'd enter $447 into Line 17.
