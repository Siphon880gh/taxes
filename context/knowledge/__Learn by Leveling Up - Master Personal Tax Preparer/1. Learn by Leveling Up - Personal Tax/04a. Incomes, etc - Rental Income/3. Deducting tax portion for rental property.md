Schedule E (Rental Income) - Line 16 (Tax)
![[Pasted image 20250414185047.png]]

For Schedule E (Rental Income) line 16, that is the portion of the property tax that you paid your county. Let’s say only 27% of your plot is rented out, so you take $4232.34 and multiply by 27%. That’s 4232.24 * .27. That equals $1,143. Notice this calculation is your total tax bill.
