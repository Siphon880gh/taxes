
A **capital loss** happens when you **sell an asset for less than what you paid for it**.

---

### 🧾 Common Examples:
- You bought stock for $5,000 and later sold it for $3,000 → you have a **$2,000 capital loss**.
- You bought cryptocurrency for $10,000, sold it for $6,000 → **$4,000 capital loss**.

---

### 💡 Key Points:
- **Capital losses can reduce your taxes** by offsetting capital gains (profits from other investments).
- If your losses are more than your gains, you can deduct **up to $3,000** ($1,500 if married filing separately) from other income (like wages).
- Any extra losses can be **carried forward** to future tax years.
