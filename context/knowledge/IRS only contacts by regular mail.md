Straight from IRS' mouth:

> If you got an unexpected call, text, email, social media message or in-person visit, **it’s not us**. [Protect yourself from scams.](https://www.irs.gov/help/tax-scams)
> 
> In almost all cases, our first contact is through regular mail delivered by the United States Postal Service. [View your mail from the IRS.](https://sa.www4.irs.gov/ola/notices_and_letters)
> 
> In extremely rare circumstances, our first contact may be an in-person visit. [Learn how to tell if it's us.](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/how-to-know-if-its-really-the-irs)
